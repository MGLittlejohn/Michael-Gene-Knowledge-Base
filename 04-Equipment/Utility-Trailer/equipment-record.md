## Capacity — Verify Before Loading
Do **not** infer payload capacity from a planned material load. Before hauling heavy aggregate or equipment, verify from the trailer data plate and actual configuration:
- GVWR
- GAWR / axle rating(s)
- tire load ratings and inflation requirements
- wheel ratings
- coupler rating
- safety-chain condition/rating
- empty trailer weight
- resulting legal payload

A prior idea involving roughly two tons of crushed material is a project target, **not** a trailer capacity rating.
