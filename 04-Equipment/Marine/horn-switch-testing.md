# Marine Horn Switch — Basic Troubleshooting

## Typical Two-Terminal Momentary Switch Test
Before testing resistance/continuity, isolate power from the horn-switch circuit as appropriate and avoid measuring continuity on a live circuit.

For a simple two-terminal normally-open momentary horn switch:
- Button released: circuit should normally be open.
- Button pressed: switch should close and show continuity / very low resistance.

If the switch behaves correctly but the horn does not sound, continue diagnosis through the fuse/breaker, power feed, ground/return path, relay if fitted, connectors, wiring, and horn itself.

## Replacement Rule
Do not order a replacement solely from appearance. Verify:
- switch function (momentary, normally open)
- terminal arrangement
- mounting-hole/panel dimensions
- current rating or relay-control duty
- marine/environmental suitability

Historical candidate part numbers are intentionally omitted until verified against the actual boat and switch.
