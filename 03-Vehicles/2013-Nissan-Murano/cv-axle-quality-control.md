# 2013 Nissan Murano FWD — CV Axle Quality-Control Checklist

Use after both front axles and inner CVT-side seals have been installed.

## Before Road Test
- Confirm both replacement axles match the removed FWD units.
- Confirm both new CVT-side seals are square and the seal lips are not rolled/cut.
- Verify inner joints/snap rings are fully seated; perform a firm tug check.
- Verify the right-side support bearing is seated naturally and its fasteners are secured to the verified specification.
- Rotate axle shafts and check for binding; verify boots are not twisted and clamps are intact.
- Restore ABS wiring and brake-hose routing/clips exactly as intended.
- Verify brake components are secure and pump the brake pedal firm before moving the vehicle.
- Torque axle nuts and wheel lugs to verified specifications and install required new retaining hardware/cotter pins.
- Correct CVT fluid level using the specified fluid and procedure.
- Check under the vehicle for tools, rags, plugs, drain pans, and leaks.

## Three-Stage Road Test
1. **Parking lot:** slow steering lock-to-lock; listen/feel for clicking, binding, or tire rub. Verify brakes.
2. **Neighborhood speed:** light acceleration and coast-down. Stop and inspect both inner-seal areas and the right support-bearing area.
3. **Normal road:** progressively load the drivetrain. Confirm the original acceleration vibration/clunk is gone.

After cool-down, recheck for CVT leakage and verify axle-nut retaining hardware and lug security.

## Stop Driving and Inspect If
- Grinding develops
- A new severe vibration appears
- CVT fluid is leaking
- ABS/VDC warning lights appear
- Brake pedal becomes soft
- An axle or support-bearing housing appears incompletely seated

## If Acceleration Vibration Remains
After verifying the axle installation, broaden diagnosis to engine/transmission mounts, control-arm bushings, ball joints, wheel bearings, wheels/tires, and engine/CVT performance before automatically condemning the new repair.
