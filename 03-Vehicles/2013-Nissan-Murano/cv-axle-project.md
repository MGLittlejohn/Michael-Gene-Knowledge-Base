# 2013 Nissan Murano FWD — Front CV Axle & Inner Seal Project

## Current Symptoms
- Front-end clunking
- Vibration during acceleration
- CV boot grease leakage

These symptoms support inspecting/replacing the front CV axles, especially with confirmed boot leakage.

## Planned Work
1. Replace driver-side front CV axle.
2. Replace passenger-side front CV axle.
3. Replace both inner CV axle/transaxle seals while both axles are removed.
4. Drain the existing CVT fluid during the repair.
5. Refill with the correct Nissan-spec CVT fluid after reassembly.
6. Inspect for leaks after the first road test and again after several heat cycles.

## Parts Status
- Front CV axles ordered from RockAuto.
- Inner axle seals to be sourced separately for driver and passenger sides.
- CVT fluid to be purchased in sufficient quantity for the planned drain/refill.

## Shop Notes
- Keep left and right axle parts identified separately during removal.
- Protect new seal lips during axle installation.
- Lubricate seal lips with clean compatible CVT fluid before axle insertion.
- Confirm axle snap-ring engagement before final assembly.
- Do not use the axle shaft as a pry or hammering surface.
- Recheck fluid level using the correct Nissan CVT service procedure after the vehicle has been run and cycled through ranges.
