**Reason for repair:**
- Front clunking

- Drained and replaced CVT fluid with new fluid
- Changed engine oil
- Replaced engine oil filter
- Cycled CVT through all ranges after refill with brakes applied, holding each position approximately 10 seconds
- Allowed vehicle to idle before final fluid check
- Completed successful road test

**September 10, 2026 morning follow-up:**
- No leaks observed under vehicle after overnight sit following road test
- CVT fluid level within XXXX marked operating zone on dipstick
- Engine oil level within operating zone on dipstick
- Plan: inspect for leaks and verify both fluid levels daily for the next several drives/heat cycles

**CV axle installation lesson:**
The splines can be engaged while the axle is still not fully seated. During this repair, the axle slid into the transaxle but required carefully controlled tapping with a driving wedge positioned to avoid the CV boot before the retaining ring fully seated and locked. Future work should distinguish between spline engagement and confirmed retaining-ring engagement.

**Torque-record lesson:**
For future repairs, final torque specifications should be vehicle-specific, component-specific, and verified before final tightening.

## September 27, 2026 — Right Front Wheel Lockup and Broken Lug Studs
**Status:** REPAIRED AND ROAD TESTED

**Symptoms:** Clunk followed by a squeal and sudden right-front wheel lockup; the wheel dragged briefly, including during a short reverse maneuver.

**Confirmed cause:** Two wheel lug studs broke. A broken stud became wedged between the hub and brake rotor, mechanically seizing the wheel. Inspection photos show the loose stud lodged inside the rotor and the missing studs at the hub.

**Service completed:** Removed the obstruction and replaced all parts damaged in the incident, per owner's report. The exact replacement parts and quantities beyond the broken studs were not recorded. Road test completed successfully.

**Follow-up:** Verify that all five right-front studs and lug nuts are sound and correctly torqued with a torque wrench; recheck after initial driving. Inspect the wheel, rotor, hub, caliper and remaining studs for damage from the lockup. Record replacement part numbers, mileage and torque values if available.
