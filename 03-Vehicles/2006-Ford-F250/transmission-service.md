# 2006 Ford F-250 — Transmission Fluid & Filter Service

## Status
**Planned / pending.**

## Service Objective
Perform a thorough transmission service rather than simply draining and refilling fluid.

### Planned Scope
- Warm vehicle appropriately before service as required by verified procedure
- Safely raise/support vehicle if additional clearance is required
- Drain/remove transmission pan using the correct procedure
- Inspect old fluid condition
- Inspect pan and magnet for abnormal debris
- Clean pan and magnet
- Replace internal pan filter
- Inspect/replace pan gasket as appropriate
- Service/replace external transmission filter
- Reinstall components using verified torque specifications and tightening sequence
- Refill with the exact specified transmission fluid
- Bring fluid to the correct checking temperature/range according to the transmission-specific procedure
- Verify final fluid level
- Check for leaks
- Road-test and recheck as appropriate

## Field Principle
The old fluid, pan, and magnet are diagnostic evidence. Inspect them before cleaning. A transmission service is an opportunity to learn what is happening inside the unit, not merely an oil change.
