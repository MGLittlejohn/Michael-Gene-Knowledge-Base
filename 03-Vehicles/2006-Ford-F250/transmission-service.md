# 2006 Ford F-250 Super Duty XLT 6.0L — 5R110W Transmission Service

## Status
**Planned / pending.**

Procedure recorded: **2026-09-11**

## Vehicle / Transmission
- Vehicle: **2006 Ford F-250 Super Duty XLT**
- Engine: **6.0L Power Stroke diesel**
- Transmission: **5R110W TorqShift automatic**
- Service scope: transmission fluid change, internal pan filter replacement, and external bypass filter replacement

## Parts & Fluids
- Internal pan filter: **Motorcraft FT-144 / Ford 3C3Z-7A098-AA**
- External bypass filter element: **Motorcraft FT-145 / Ford 3C3Z-7B155-BA**
- Fluid: **MERCON SP**
- Pan gasket: inspect factory reusable gasket; replace if damaged, distorted, hardened, or leaking
- Have approximately **10–12 quarts** of MERCON SP available for a normal pan/filter service
- Published approximate dry-fill capacity is about **17.5 quarts**, but this is **not** the amount to automatically add after a pan service

## Useful Tools / Sizes
- Large drain pan, preferably graduated so drained fluid can be measured
- 13 mm socket for pan drain plug
- 10 mm socket for transmission pan bolts
- 22 mm **six-point** socket for external filter housing
- Low-range torque wrench / inch-pound torque wrench
- Small pick for removing an old filter-neck seal if it remains in the bore

# Step-by-Step Procedure

## Phase 1 — Drain the Transmission

1. Park the truck on a **level surface**.
2. Set the parking brake and chock the wheels.
3. Keep the truck level whenever possible. Raise/support it only if additional clearance is needed.
4. Place a large drain pan under the transmission.
5. Remove the transmission pan drain plug with a **13 mm socket**.
6. Allow the fluid to drain thoroughly.
7. **Measure and record the amount drained.** This becomes the starting reference for refill quantity.
8. Inspect the drained fluid before disposal:
   - Color
   - Burnt odor
   - Visible metallic material
   - Friction material or unusual contamination

### Drain Plug
- Reinstall torque: approximately **18 lb-ft**.

---

## Phase 2 — Remove Pan and Replace Internal Filter

1. The transmission pan uses **20 bolts** with 10 mm heads.
2. Remove most bolts first, leaving one loose bolt on opposite sides to control the pan while lowering it.
3. Support the pan, remove the final bolts, and lower the pan carefully.
4. Inspect the pan and magnet **before cleaning**.

### Normal / Abnormal Debris
Normal:
- Fine gray metallic paste or very fine fuzz on the magnet

Investigate before continuing if there are:
- Shiny flakes
- Large metal particles
- Bronze/brass-looking material
- Heavy clutch/friction debris
- Chunks or unusual hard material

5. Pull the internal **FT-144** filter straight downward, rocking slightly side-to-side if needed.
6. **Critical check:** make sure the old filter-neck O-ring/seal comes out with the old filter.
7. If the old seal remains stuck in the transmission bore, remove it carefully with a small pick.
8. Clean the transmission pan and magnet thoroughly. Avoid leaving lint, grit, gasket debris, or solvent residue.
9. Lightly coat the new FT-144 filter O-ring with fresh MERCON SP.
10. Push the new filter squarely into the bore until fully seated.
11. Inspect the pan gasket. Reuse only if flat, flexible, undamaged, and sealing properly.
12. Reinstall the pan and start all bolts by hand.
13. Tighten pan bolts gradually in a crisscross pattern.

### Pan Bolt Torque
- Approximately **11 lb-ft / 132 in-lb**.

### Important Warning
Do **not** leave the old filter O-ring in the transmission bore and then force the new filter into it.

---

## Phase 3 — Replace External Bypass Filter

### Location
- Under the truck on the **passenger side**
- Along/inside the **passenger-side frame rail**
- In the transmission cooler circuit
- Cylindrical metal housing with a hex on the cap/housing

1. Place a drain pan directly under the external filter housing.
2. Use a **22 mm six-point socket** on the housing.
3. Unscrew the housing carefully. Expect some fluid loss.
4. Pay attention to the order/orientation of the internal pieces as they come out.
5. Remove the old **FT-145** filter element.
6. Remove and discard the old housing O-ring.
7. Clean the magnetic base carefully.
8. Avoid aggressive solvents on plastic-covered magnetic parts. Do not soak or spray them with harsh brake/carb cleaner.
9. Install the new FT-145 element in the same orientation.
10. Lightly coat the new O-ring with MERCON SP.
11. Reassemble the spring, filter element, magnetic base, and housing in their original orientation.
12. Start the housing completely **by hand** before using the socket to avoid cross-threading.
13. Tighten securely without overloading or twisting the mounting bracket.

### External Filter Watch-Outs
- Use a proper **six-point socket** rather than an adjustable wrench.
- Do not use excessive leverage on the housing or bracket.
- Replace the housing O-ring every time the filter is serviced.

---

## Phase 4 — Refill and Level Check

1. Add up all fluid drained from:
   - Pan/drain plug
   - Fluid remaining in the pan
   - External filter housing
2. Use the measured drained amount as the **starting refill reference**.
3. Refill through the transmission dipstick tube with **MERCON SP**.
4. Initially add about **one quart less than the measured amount removed**.
5. Start the engine.
6. With the brake firmly applied, slowly move the selector through:
   - P
   - R
   - N
   - D
   - Lower ranges
   - Back to P
7. Pause several seconds in each range.
8. Leave the engine running in **PARK**.
9. Inspect for leaks at:
   - Pan perimeter
   - Drain plug
   - External filter housing
   - Cooler-line connections in the filter area
10. Bring the transmission to normal checking temperature, approximately **150–170°F transmission-fluid temperature**.
11. Park on level ground with engine running in PARK.
12. Cycle through the gear ranges once more and return to PARK.
13. Pull the dipstick, wipe clean, fully reinstall, then pull again to read.
14. Add MERCON SP only in **small increments** until the fluid is in the proper HOT operating range.
15. Do not overfill.
16. Road-test the truck and recheck for leaks and final level.

# Key Field Notes

## 1. Internal Filter O-Ring
Make absolutely certain the old seal is not left stuck in the transmission bore.

## 2. External Filter Housing
Use the correct 22 mm six-point socket, start the housing by hand, and avoid stressing the bracket.

## 3. Refill Quantity
Do **not** blindly refill the published approximate **17.5-quart dry capacity** after a pan service. Measure what came out, refill conservatively, then use the dipstick at the correct fluid temperature for the final level.

## 4. Pan / Magnet Inspection
The old fluid, pan, and magnet are diagnostic evidence. Inspect them before cleaning. Fine gray paste is common; chunks, flakes, bronze/brass material, or heavy friction debris deserve investigation.
