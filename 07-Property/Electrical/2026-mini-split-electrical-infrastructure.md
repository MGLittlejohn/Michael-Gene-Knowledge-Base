## Panel / Breaker Lesson
A 240 V load requires the two-pole breaker to connect to both opposing bus legs. A physically narrow breaker arrangement that does not land on both legs will not provide the required 240 V.

A GE/ABB-family panel discussion during the project distinguished the required two-pole 15 A breaker arrangement from a tandem-style 15/15 breaker that did not serve the intended 240 V load.

## Safety / Documentation Rule
For permanent reference, use the actual panel labeling, breaker listing, conductor size, equipment nameplate MCA/MOCP, and manufacturer instructions. Do not infer breaker compatibility merely because a breaker appears to fit physically.
