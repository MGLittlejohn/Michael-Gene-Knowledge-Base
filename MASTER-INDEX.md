## Dedicated Technical Library

### HVAC Knowledge Base
The separate `HVAC-Knowledge-Base` is the specialized technical library for residential HVAC, commercial HVAC, MVAC, heat pumps, refrigeration, field procedures, troubleshooting, service reports, diagrams, and teaching references.

## Filing Rule
Put information where you would naturally look for it later. Use the master library for the broad personal record and the dedicated HVAC repository for polished technical HVAC/MVAC knowledge. Cross-reference rather than unnecessarily duplicating large documents.

## Working Rule
New material can be captured from ongoing work and then filed into the appropriate category. Existing material should not be deleted or substantially reorganized without review.
