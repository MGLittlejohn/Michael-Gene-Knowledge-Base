## Maintenance Rule
When new information arrives:
1. File durable facts in the appropriate permanent category.
2. Put detailed HVAC/MVAC technical material in the private HVAC repository.
3. Mark uncertain specifications as **VERIFY** rather than guessing.
4. Avoid duplicate copies when a cross-reference will do.
5. Do not delete or substantially reorganize existing material without deliberate review.
