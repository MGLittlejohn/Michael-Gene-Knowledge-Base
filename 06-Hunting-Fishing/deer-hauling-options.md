# Deer Hauling Options — Historical Shortlist

## Purpose
Reference notes from prior research into easier ways to move harvested deer and other game across property.

## Previously Considered Options
- Jet Sled 1
- DEER Sleigh'r Magnum
- Terrain Deer Drag Sled
- Beavertail Magnum XT Cargo Sled Package
- 550-lb-class polyethylene utility sleds
- Game Glide

## How to Compare Them
Evaluate:
- empty weight and packability
- terrain and brush resistance
- width through woods and gates
- towing/dragging method
- actual rated load or manufacturer guidance
- durability of runners/bottom
- ease of cleaning
- current price and availability

## Historical Observation
Game Glide was considered more of a lightweight drag-assist approach, while rigid polyethylene sleds provide more structure and can serve broader property-hauling jobs.

## Verification Rule
This is a historical shortlist, not a current buying recommendation. Verify present model specifications, capacity, availability, and price before purchase or use.
