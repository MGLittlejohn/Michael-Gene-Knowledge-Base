# Shop

This section is for reusable shop knowledge rather than records for one particular vehicle, machine, or property project.

## Put Here
- General hand-tool and power-tool notes
- Measuring/testing tools
- Fastener and driver identification
- Fabrication and repair techniques
- Shop organization
- Reusable safety/inspection checklists
- General procedures that apply across several projects

## Do Not Duplicate
- Vehicle-specific procedures belong in `03-Vehicles/`.
- Equipment-specific maintenance belongs in `04-Equipment/`.
- Property-specific work belongs in `07-Property/`.
- Computer/technology work belongs in `09-Technology/`.
- Manufacturer manuals belong in `11-Manuals/` or, when practical, alongside the equipment they document.
- Blank reusable forms belong in `12-Templates/`.
- Active cross-category jobs belong in `13-Projects/`.
- Detailed HVAC technical material belongs in the private `HVAC-Knowledge-Base`.

## Working Principle
A shop note should teach a reusable skill. A project record should tell the story of a particular job.
