# Shipment Receiving & Damage Inspection Template

## Before Opening
- Photograph all sides of the unopened package.
- Photograph shipping labels and any crushed, torn, punctured, wet, or otherwise damaged areas.
- Record delivery date/time.
- If practical, record the unboxing continuously.

## During Unboxing
- Open carefully without destroying packaging that may be needed for a claim.
- Photograph packing arrangement before removing items.
- Compare contents with the packing list/order.
- Photograph each major item and accessory.
- Do not repair, straighten, or modify suspected shipping damage before documenting it.

## If Damage or Missing Parts Are Found
- Photograph the issue from several angles, including close-up and full-item views.
- Where possible, photograph the relationship between exterior package damage and product damage.
- Preserve original photos/video without unnecessary editing.
- Keep the product, every part, box, packing material, and shipping label until the claim/refund/replacement is complete.
- Record the seller/carrier case number, representative, date, and time.

## If Everything Is Good
- Take a final photograph showing the complete item and included accessories.
- Keep the box and packing until the item has been inspected and tested.

## Rule to Remember
**Photograph first. Open second. Discard nothing until the transaction is settled.**
