## Wisdom & Discernment

### Sometimes Wisdom Is Knowing What to Check Next

> "Sometimes wisdom looks less like having every answer and more like knowing what to check next."

> "Wisdom isn't omniscience. Wisdom is discernment about the next faithful step."

**Thought:** Wisdom does not always arrive carrying an answer. Sometimes it arrives carrying the right question.

---

### A Symptom Is Not a Diagnosis

> "A symptom deserves attention, but it hasn't earned the right to become the diagnosis."

> "A symptom is evidence. It is not necessarily the verdict."

**Thought:** What we first notice may be real without being the whole truth. Good troubleshooting and good discernment both require us to investigate before reaching a conclusion.

---

### Assumptions Are Not Facts

> "Discernment begins when we stop letting assumptions masquerade as facts."

**Thought:** Before reacting to what we think is happening, slow down and examine what we actually know.

---

### Check the Evidence

**Proverbs 14:15 (ESV)**

> "The simple believes everything, but the prudent gives thought to his steps."

**Application:**

Check the evidence.  
Question the assumption.  
Ask God for wisdom.  
Then take the next step.

Sometimes faith isn't knowing every answer. Sometimes faith is trusting God enough to slow down and make sure you're asking the right question.

---

### Don't Change Everything Because One Thing Isn't Working

> "Don't change everything just because one thing isn't working. Sometimes wisdom means staying put and making a small adjustment. That works on the water. It works in relationships. It works in life."

---

## Porch Thought 🌾

Troubleshooting equipment and troubleshooting life have something in common:

**Don't confuse what you noticed first with what is actually wrong.**

Observe. Test. Think. Pray. Then act.

## August 9, 2026 — Discernment, Evidence & Assumptions

### Absence of Evidence

> "Absence of evidence is not always evidence of absence."

**Thought:** Sometimes the thing you're looking for is there. You just haven't found the right way to see it yet.

### Facts and Conclusions

> "A fact can be true while the conclusion we draw from it is false."

**Thought:** What happened and what we believe it means are not always the same thing. Wisdom learns to separate observation from interpretation.

### Wisdom Examines the Assumption

> "Wisdom doesn't merely ask, 'What happened?' Wisdom also asks, 'What am I assuming about what happened?'"

**Thought:** Discernment slows down long enough to distinguish evidence from the story our minds build around it.

### Four Questions Before Reacting

Before reacting, ask:

1. What do I actually know?
2. What am I assuming?
3. What else could explain this?
4. What response would honor Christ even if my assumption is wrong?

### Worth Remembering

> "Sometimes we never learn exactly why somebody did what they did. But we can still decide who we're going to be in response."

> "Sometimes we never learn exactly why somebody did what they did. We can still choose a response that reflects who Christ is making us."

> "I don't need every mystery explained before I can behave wisely."

**Thought:** Christian maturity does not require us to understand another person's motives before choosing a Christ-honoring response.

## Grace, Sanctification & Spiritual Maturity

### Grace Ain't a Spare Tire

**Romans 6:14**

> "You pursue holiness because in Christ, God already has."

Sanctification is the lifelong work of God conforming His people to Christ. Don't confuse slow transformation with no transformation.

> "The Christian doesn't fight sin trying to earn a victory. He fights from a victory Christ has already won."

Maturity does not make grace less necessary. It gives us clearer sight of how desperately we need it.

---

### Grace Will Teach You What Guilt Never Could

**Titus 2:11–12**

Grace trains us. Conviction points us toward Christ; condemnation merely points at us.

> "Grace doesn't merely pardon the sinner. Grace begins retraining the desires."

> "Repentance isn't crawling back. Christ has already opened the way home."

**Application:** Recognize it. Name it. Receive Christ's forgiveness. Then take the next obedient step.

---

### Spiritual Maturity Makes Grace More Precious

> "His growth didn't make grace obsolete. It made grace increasingly precious."

> "Spiritual maturity isn't outgrowing your need for grace. It's finally growing enough to understand how much you've always needed it."

> "More maturity → clearer sight → greater awareness of dependence → grace becomes more precious."

> "The mature Christian doesn't graduate from grace. He discovers just how deep grace goes."

---

### Conviction, Repentance & Grace

> "Conviction identifies the rut. Repentance turns the wheel. Grace supplies the strength to climb out."

**Theme:** conviction, repentance, grace, behavior patterns, sanctification, changing direction, Christian psychology.

---

### Grace Does Not Lower the Standard

> "Grace isn't God lowering the standard until our old behavior fits. Grace is God supplying what we could never manufacture."

Grace does not make transformation unnecessary. Grace makes transformation possible.

## Becoming & Turning Northward

### Kierkegaard and the Problem of Becoming

The Christian philosopher Søren Kierkegaard spent considerable thought on what it means to become a self before God.

> "Christianity isn't merely about possessing correct ideas. It's about becoming a particular kind of person through relationship with God."

Knowing which direction is north isn't the same thing as walking north. Christian formation involves movement, not merely information.

---

### Turn Northward

You don't have to conquer every weakness today.
You don't have to understand every problem today.
And you certainly don't have to become good enough to stop needing Jesus today.

Your assignment is smaller:

**Recognize → repent → receive grace → respond differently.**

Then take one faithful step north.

> "Enormous mountains sometimes get conquered not with dramatic spiritual heroics, but with thousands of little decisions pointed north."

---

## Working Principle

Events tell you what happened. Patterns begin telling you why.

**Trigger → thought → feeling → reaction → consequence.**

Sanctification means God works, and we participate. Growth is neither self-salvation nor passive resignation. It is dependent, grace-powered obedience.
