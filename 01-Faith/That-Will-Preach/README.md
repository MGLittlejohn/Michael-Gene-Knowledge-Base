# That Will Preach

A collection of Scripture, spiritual insights, practical theology,
Christian psychology, behavior patterns, country wisdom, and memorable
thoughts worth keeping.

## How This Collection Works

When Michael Gene says:

- "That will preach"
- "Mark that"
- "This is an important takeaway"

the thought is considered a keeper and may be added to this collection.

## Categories

- Scripture
- Practical Theology
- Christian Psychology
- Behavior Patterns
- Porch Thoughts
- Life Lessons
- Teaching & Sunday School
- Memorable One-Liners

## Guiding Thought

Some truths deserve more than a passing read.
They deserve to be marked, remembered, lived, and shared.
